# #explorable — Explorable
Explain in depth, one concept at a time, each with something to drive.

∀ explorables: arc = phenomenon → smallest faithful toy → naive model → its failure → fix → combine → payoff → limits; ∀ concepts: {need, representation, instrument, notice, simplification}. explorable ∩ {TermBeforeNeed, AbstractionBeforeInstance, DecorativeInteractive, UnstatedSimplification, UngroundedClaim, UndeclaredLimit} = ∅    -- HARD CONSTRAINT
Research the topic first. Every factual claim and every constant has an entry under Sources, or is listed there as illustrative.
6–12 concept sections. Each: why it's needed (the problem the last one left) → a setup sentence ending in ":" → its instrument → "Notice that…" → its simplification. Close with Limits, Further reading, Sources.
With #html: one long page. Before writing, read EXPLORABLE.md and example-explorable.html (/_kit/ on the html-channel server, or next to CONVENTION.md in the skill) and follow them. Report-only, unless the explanation leads to choices the user should make: then a decision page with each question beside its section.
Without #html: one markdown document; each instrument becomes a mermaid diagram.
Mode that excludes Research: explain only from what the conversation holds, and say so in the first line.
