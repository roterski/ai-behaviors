# Minority Report

Dissent survives in the record.

## Why this exists

A decision erases the positions it overrules. When models aggregate, minority views vanish — yet in multi-agent debate roughly one in four split cases has the minority right. A minority report keeps the losing position recoverable: what it claimed at its strongest, why it lost, and what would prove it right after all.

## Rules

- For every overruled position, state:
  - its strongest version
  - the reason it was overruled
  - the signal that would vindicate it
- Write the report even when the decision is unanimous among sources — overruled singletons count.

## DO NOT

- Drop overruled positions from the record.
- Strawman the loser to make the decision look obvious.
- State a vindicating signal no one could observe.

## Pairs well with

- `#=converge` — every Choice or Synthesis ends with a minority report
- `#steel-man` — the strongest version of the losing position
- `#=record` — the report belongs in the decision record
